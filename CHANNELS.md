## Economics (why topics must monetize off-platform)

- YouTube Shorts pay ~$0.03–0.35 per 1,000 views (finance top, education bottom) → 1M views ≈ $30–350.
  Shorts = discovery. Revenue = affiliate links / lead magnets / products per channel.
- TikTok Creator Rewards pays only for videos **> 60s** (~$0.40–2 RPM; 10k followers + 100k views/30d).
  → every short ships two cuts: **40s** (Shorts/Reels) and **61–75s** (TikTok).
- YouTube's "inauthentic content" policy (clarified Jul 2026) judges whole channels, cumulatively.
  → vary formats inside a channel, every video carries a non-obvious insight, no AI "expert"
  persona on money topics: we *show the math*, never give advice.
